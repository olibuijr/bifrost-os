## Light, Dark or Auto {#mode}
Appearance chooses how the desktop looks. Light and Dark keep the same mode all the time. Auto uses Light from 07:00 to 19:00 and Dark at other hours. The change fades over about a third of a second.

## Fonts {#fonts}
The Fonts page lists the fonts installed for the system. Apps use the system text styles, so a font change applies to the whole desktop.
