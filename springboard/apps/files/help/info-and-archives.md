## Get Info {#get-info}
Get Info shows the kind, size, dates and the owner and permissions of an item. For a picture it also shows the dimensions.

## Open With {#open-with}
Open With chooses the app that opens the selected file. Open With... lists every app that can open the file type, and the one you choose opens it.

## Preview {#preview}
Press Space, or choose Preview, to see a file without opening its app. Press Escape or click to close the preview.

## Compress {#compress}
Compress makes an archive of the selected items. Compress as TAR.GZ or Compress as TAR.XZ chooses the format. A compressing job can be cancelled from the progress panel; the archive is then not written.

## Extract an archive {#extract}
Open an archive to unpack its contents into a folder beside it. The progress panel shows the job, and Cancel leaves the folder as it was.
