## Browse your files {#browse}
Files shows the folders of your account. Double click a folder to open it, and use Back and Forward in the toolbar to move through the folders you visited.

## The sidebar {#sidebar}
The sidebar lists Favorites (Home, Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Music, Pictures, Videos and Projects), Recents, Locations and Tags. Choose Show Sidebar or Hide Sidebar in the View menu to show or hide it.

## Change the view {#views}
Show the items as icons or as a list. Show View Options sets the sort order, and Group By None, Group By Kind or Group By Date groups the items. Show Status Bar shows the item count and the free space.

## Show hidden files {#hidden}
Show Hidden Files shows the items whose names start with a dot. Hide Hidden Files puts them back out of view.

## Find items {#search}
Type in the Search field to look for a name in the folder you are in. Use Find to search from the top of the search field.
