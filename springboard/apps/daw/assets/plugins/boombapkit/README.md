# Boom Bap Kit

Dusty 90s boom-bap kit: SP-1200-style crunchy kick, cracking snare, crisp short hats (real drums, 12-bit). 85-95 BPM, swing 0.1-0.25: kick on 1 & the "and" of 2/3, snare 2 & 4, 8th/16th hats with velocity accents. Notes: 36 kick, 38 snare, 42 hat, 46 open, 39 clap, 37 rim.
